# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Bestdecks contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- giving specific, evidence-based feedback;
- assuming good faith while challenging technical claims;
- respecting different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and communication styles;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting the record;
- protecting private reports, credentials, customer data, and personal information;
- focusing disagreement on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private communications, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community spaces, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the verified contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in an issue.

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this policy. They may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants when necessary to protect the community. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, avoid conflicts of interest, and explain enforcement decisions to affected parties when safe and practical.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is not permitted.
