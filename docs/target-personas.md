# Target-user hypotheses

Status: discovery hypotheses, not validated market claims or a pricing plan.

Bestdecks is most plausibly useful to people who already create tailored B2B proposals and can
evaluate whether source-linked research improves their workflow. Initial discovery should focus
on these groups:

| Hypothesis | Existing job | Question to validate |
|---|---|---|
| Outbound lead | Prepares follow-up material for selected prospects | Is a deck useful at this stage, and what evidence must it contain? |
| Agency founder | Creates bespoke proposals for prospective clients | Which parts of research and drafting are repetitive versus judgment-heavy? |
| Independent consultant | Reuses an offer across different buyer contexts | Does a local BYOK workflow fit their security and cost expectations? |
| Account executive | Tailors material for active opportunities | Where should human review remain mandatory? |
| Services BD lead | Coordinates research and proposal inputs | Which approvals and audit records would a later team product require? |

## Discovery protocol

For each group:

1. Observe the current workflow before presenting Bestdecks.
2. Record inputs, review steps, failure cost, and the artifact actually sent.
3. Run the reference pipeline only with approved public targets and synthetic or authorized seller
   data.
4. Ask the participant to identify unsupported, irrelevant, or missing claims.
5. Retain consented notes separately from the public repository.

Do not infer willingness to pay, time savings, conversion lift, market size, or required scale from
these interviews. Hosted pricing remains an internal hypothesis until the controlled cost
benchmark and actual product boundaries support it.

## Evidence needed before narrowing the market

- repeated use by the same workflow, not a one-time demo reaction;
- reviewed run receipts and evidence-coverage results;
- observed failure and correction patterns;
- measured provider cost and latency across at least 30 completed reference runs; and
- a clear reason the generated deck is preferable to a brief, email, or existing template.
